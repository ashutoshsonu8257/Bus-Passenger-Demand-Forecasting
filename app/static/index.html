Responsible AI, Decision Support Boundaries & Limitations

1. Human-in-the-Loop Dispatch Rule: This application is strictly an advisory decision-support system. Fleet dispatchers must manually confirm driver Hours-of-Service limits, vehicle maintenance readiness, and depot availability before adding or merging scheduled departures.
2. Cold-Start New Routes: Brand-new bus corridors without 7-day or 14-day historical lag data fall back to Route Type averages and D-1 booking pace; forecasts carry wider uncertainty intervals until 2+ weeks of ridership history accumulate.
3. Acute Unplanned Disruptions (<24h Horizon): Sudden highway blockades, flash floods, or transit strikes occurring inside the 24-hour window after the D-1 18:00 cut-off cannot be inferred by historical booking curves and require manual operator override.
4. Zero PII & Zero Target Leakage: Only aggregate trip counts and D-1 booking totals are processed. No passenger names, phone numbers, or payment credentials are ingested or stored.
